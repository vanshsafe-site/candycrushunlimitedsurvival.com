How to Play

  • Click one candy, then click an adjacent candy to swap them.
  • Match 3 or more of the same color to score points.
  • Special matches will create power-ups:
  • Match 4 in a row: Creates a striped candy that clears an entire row or column.
  • Match 5 in a row: Creates a color bomb that clears all candies of the same color.
  • Complete the target score within the limited moves to advance to the next level.
  • Click the Hint button if you're stuck!
